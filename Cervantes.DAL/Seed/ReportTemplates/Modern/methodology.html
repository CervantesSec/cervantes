Methodology

The assessment combined automated scanning with manual testing. Testing followed recognised industry guidance (OWASP Testing Guides, PTES and NIST SP 800-115) and was adapted to the agreed scope.

ActivityWhat it covers
ReconnaissanceGathering public and provided information about the targets, technologies and exposed services.
DiscoveryMapping the attack surface: hosts, ports, endpoints, roles and data flows.
ExploitationConfirming vulnerabilities by safely demonstrating their impact.
Post-exploitationAssessing what an attacker could reach from a compromised position.
ReportingDocumenting every finding with evidence, a risk rating and a remediation.

Risk rating

Each finding is rated with CVSS 3.1. The rating sets the order in which findings should be addressed.

RiskCVSSMeaning
Critical9.0 - 10.0Direct compromise of systems or data is likely. Fix immediately.
High7.0 - 8.9Serious impact with limited preconditions. Fix as a priority.
Medium4.0 - 6.9Meaningful impact that needs specific conditions. Plan a fix.
Low0.1 - 3.9Limited impact. Fix during regular maintenance.
Info0.0No direct risk. Good-practice observation.
